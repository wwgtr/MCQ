💡 Study Suggestions & Exam Tips

  • Vocabulary Upgrades: Focus heavily on transforming informal words into formal alternatives (e.g., want → request, tell → inform, get → obtain) as these are highly expected in administrative settings. [cite: 23]
  • Preposition Precision: Remember key combinations like "responsible for", "in charge of", and "report to". They are standard parts of workplace expressions. [cite: 8]
  • Email Layout: Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences) for optimal digital readability, and never omit the concise 4-part structure (Subject, Greeting, Body, Closing). [cite: 27]
  • Financial Formulas: Keep the basic conditions in mind: Profit requires Income > Expenses, while Loss occurs when Expenses > Income. [cite: 7]
